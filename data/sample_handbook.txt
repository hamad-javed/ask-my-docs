Brightleaf Studios Employee Handbook

Office Hours and Access
The Brightleaf office at 14 Harbour Lane is open from 7:30am to 7:00pm, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00am to 3:30pm, and all staff are expected to be available during this window, either in the office or online. The building is closed at weekends; staff who need weekend access must request a temporary pass from Facilities at least two working days in advance.

Remote Working
Staff may work from home up to three days per week. Wednesday is the company-wide "anchor day", when everyone is expected in the office for team meetings and the monthly all-hands. Anyone working abroad for more than ten consecutive working days must get written approval from their manager and the People team, for tax reasons.

Holidays and Leave
Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year, but carried-over days must be used by the end of March or they are lost. Every employee also gets their birthday off as an extra paid day. Holiday requests of more than five consecutive days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Sick Leave
If you are unwell, message your manager before 9:30am on the first day of absence. For absences of up to seven calendar days you can self-certify through the HR portal. Absences longer than seven days require a fit note from a doctor.

Expenses
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase, with a photo of the receipt attached, using the Expensify app. Meals while travelling on company business are covered up to £35 per day. Train travel should be booked in standard class; first class is only allowed for journeys longer than three hours. Taxis are reimbursed only after 9:00pm or when carrying heavy equipment.

Equipment and IT
New starters receive a laptop and one external monitor on their first day. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours by emailing it-help@brightleaf.example. Personal software may not be installed on company laptops without IT approval. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must not be reused.

Kitchen and Facilities
The office kitchen is stocked with free fruit on Mondays and Thursdays. The coffee machine is serviced every Tuesday morning between 8:00am and 11:00am and cannot be used during that time. The fridge is cleared every Friday at 4:00pm, and anything left inside is thrown away. Bicycles can be stored in the basement bike room, which is opened with your staff pass.

Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of £1,200, which can be spent on courses, books, conferences or certifications. Spending over £500 on a single item requires approval from your manager. Employees may also take up to three paid study days per year to prepare for exams.
